1. Scope

This policy explains how the operator handles information for Aurora Stack. TikTok, advertising services and the website host may process information independently under their own policies. This policy does not replace those policies.

2. Information in the current build

Device-local game data: level and chapter progress, scores, stars, medals, building and decoration progress, tutorial status, gameplay rewards and preferences such as language, music and sound settings. The Game stores this through local device storage, using the key aurora-stack-v1, to remember your progress and settings. The reviewed build does not upload this save to an operator backend.

Support communications: if you contact us, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to send. We use it to respond and investigate. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

The reviewed local build does not implement an operator account system, cloud saves, a standalone analytics SDK, or access to contacts, precise location, camera or microphone. It does not currently retrieve your TikTok profile through a login integration. If these features are introduced, this policy will be updated before the new processing begins.

3. Platform and advertising services

The Game includes integration points for TikTok-provided rewarded and interstitial ads where available and configured. It uses ad availability and completion callbacks to decide whether to show an ad or grant a gameplay reward. These callbacks are not an operator-created advertising profile.

TikTok and its advertising services may process device, network, account and ad interaction information under the policies applicable to your region and your platform settings. Review the privacy information available within TikTok or on TikTok’s legal pages. The operator has not independently verified all platform processing and does not claim that the platform collects no personal information.

The reviewed build does not send save data to third-party advertisers through a separate operator integration. If TikTok login, cloud storage, analytics or additional providers are introduced, we will update this policy with the actual data, recipients, purposes and retention before that processing begins, and obtain permission where required.

4. Purposes and legal bases

We use local data to deliver the Game and remember your settings, and support correspondence to answer requests and resolve issues. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing necessary to provide a requested service relies on contract necessity; reasonable service protection and support may rely on legitimate interests, subject to your rights. Consent will be requested for optional processing where required. Legal obligations may require us to retain certain records.

5. Sharing and hosting

We do not sell device-local game saves or upload them for targeted advertising in the reviewed build. A user’s support message may be processed by the email service used to handle it. Information may be disclosed where legally required or necessary to protect legal rights, with applicable safeguards. The host serving these legal pages may receive ordinary request information, such as IP address and browser details, under its own policy; the reviewed legal pages contain no added analytics scripts.

6. Retention and deletion

Device-local saves remain until cleared by you, the browser or the host platform. Clearing the Game’s local data removes local progress and settings and may be irreversible. We cannot recover device-only saves from a server. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to address the request and meet applicable legal obligations, then delete it or remove identifying details. Platform-held data follows the platform’s retention rules.

7. Your choices and rights

You can change Game settings, decline optional rewarded ads, stop playing, and manage platform ad/privacy controls where available. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a supervisory authority. Contact agd3185@gmail.com to exercise applicable rights. We may reasonably verify your request; we cannot access device-only saves remotely or delete information held independently by TikTok.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children below the applicable platform minimum age. We do not knowingly solicit their personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact agd3185@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. Security and international processing

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle; no system is completely secure. Local saves depend on device and platform security. Email, hosting and platform providers may process information in other countries under their own arrangements. Before introducing operator-controlled international transfers, we will identify the actual arrangements and apply safeguards required by applicable law.

10. Changes and contact

We will revise this policy when practices change and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required. Contact the operator at agd3185@gmail.com for privacy questions.
